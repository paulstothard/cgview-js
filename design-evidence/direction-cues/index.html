Feature direction cues at base-pair detail

Proposed sparse, feature-colored chevrons for preserving strand orientation when an arrowhead is outside the viewport.

Direction without the endpoint

Repeated cues retain forward or reverse orientation when zooming leaves the terminal feature arrowhead off-screen.

Feature-derived styling

The stroke is calculated from the feature color and kept translucent enough to remain subordinate to names and sequence data.

Geometric consistency

Chevron arms use the same tangent-to-radial slope as the feature's configured arrowhead.

Label-aware spacing

When an inline label is accepted, markers repeat outward symmetrically from a protected text gutter.

Stable while panning

Unlabelled patterns are anchored to map coordinates rather than the clipped viewport boundary.

Bounded cost

Cues are skipped in fast draws, without sequence data, below readable base detail, and in tracks too thin to show them clearly.
